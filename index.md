# Find your place. Keep your flow.

Crumbs is a quiet in-page outline and Markdown reader for Chrome. It helps you navigate long, structured pages without replacing the page or turning your browser into a dashboard.

## What Crumbs does

- Builds a searchable outline from visible page headings.
- Tracks your current section as you read.
- Jumps directly to headings.
- Docks on the left or right and collapses into a narrow rail.
- Turns eligible standalone Markdown documents into a readable view.
- Renders tables, task lists, highlighted code, Mermaid diagrams, math, and more.
- Keeps settings and document view preferences on your device.

## Support

For help or privacy questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/k-veloso/crumbs-help/issues). Do not include passwords, private documents, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
