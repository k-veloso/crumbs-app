<h2 id="reading-title">Made for reading, at your pace.</h2>

Crumbs builds a searchable outline from visible page headings, tracks your current section, and lets you jump directly to what you need. Dock it on either side, pin it open, or collapse it into a narrow rail.

The Markdown reader supports tables, task lists, highlighted code, Mermaid diagrams, math, and more. Settings and document view preferences stay on your device.

Crumbs is a reader, not an editor. JSON reading and AI-chat-specific outlines are not currently available.
