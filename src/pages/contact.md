# Contact us

Need a hand with Crumbs, or have a privacy question? Email [karl.ext.dev@gmail.com](mailto:karl.ext.dev@gmail.com) or use the public issue tracker below.

## Email

Contact [karl.ext.dev@gmail.com](mailto:karl.ext.dev@gmail.com) for support or privacy questions. Include only the information needed to explain your question; do not send passwords or other sensitive personal information.

## GitHub

[Open an issue](https://github.com/k-veloso/crumbs-app/issues) to report a problem or ask a question.

Issues are public. Do not include passwords, private documents, health or financial information, or other sensitive personal information.

## What to include

For a problem with the extension, tell us your Chrome version, the kind of page or document you were reading, what you expected, and what happened. Share a public example URL if it helps explain the problem.
