# Contact us

Report a problem with Crumbs, such as missing headings on a page. You can also ask for help, suggest a feature, or ask a privacy question.

## Email

Contact [karl.ext.dev@gmail.com](mailto:karl.ext.dev@gmail.com) for support or privacy questions. Include only the information needed to explain your question; do not send passwords or other sensitive personal information.

## GitHub

[Open an issue](https://github.com/k-veloso/crumbs-app/issues/new/choose) to report a problem, suggest a feature, or ask a question.

Issues are public. Do not include passwords, private documents, health or financial information, or other sensitive personal information.

## What to include

For a problem with the extension, tell us your Chrome version, the kind of page or document you were reading, what you expected, and what happened. Share a public example URL if it helps explain the problem.

## Feature requests

Have an idea for Crumbs? [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/k-veloso/crumbs-app/issues/new/choose) or email [karl.ext.dev@gmail.com](mailto:karl.ext.dev@gmail.com). Tell us what you’d like to do and how it would help your reading workflow. GitHub issues are public.
