## Product basics

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Chrome 127 or newer" icon="chrome">
    Crumbs requires Chrome 127 or newer. Refresh pages that were already open
    when the extension was installed or updated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local by design" icon="padlock">
    Page headings, ChatGPT outlines, Markdown and JSON content, and preferences
    are handled on your device. Crumbs has no account system, analytics,
    advertising, or developer-operated backend.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
