# Getting started

After installing Crumbs, open a page with headings. A narrow rail appears at the edge of supported pages.

## Navigate a page

1. Expand the rail to see the page outline.
2. Select a heading to jump to that section.
3. Use search to narrow the outline by heading text.
4. Filter by heading level when you only want the document's major sections.
5. Pin the outline to keep it expanded, or collapse it when you want more room.

Crumbs tracks the section you are currently reading. You can dock the rail on either side of the page.

## Choose where Crumbs appears

Open the Crumbs toolbar button and choose one of the site modes:

- **All sites** shows Crumbs wherever a supported page is available.
- **Specific sites** limits Crumbs to domains and URL patterns you add.
- **Except these** shows Crumbs everywhere except your listed sites.

The quick controls for jumping to the top or bottom and changing the dock side can each be shown or hidden.

## Appearance

- **Glazed** adapts to the page with a quiet translucent appearance.
- **Plain** provides solid Light, Dark, and System themes.

Choose from Caramel, Ube, Blueberry, Velvet, Custard, and Matcha accents.
